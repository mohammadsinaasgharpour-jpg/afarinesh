# Seed for MiroFish: how Shiraz parents react to the new Afarinesh school website

Everything below is taken from the live site (afarinesh-site.vercel.app, October 2026) and the school's public Google Maps listing. Nothing here is invented.

## The decision being simulated

Parents in Shiraz, Iran, choosing a private (non-governmental) lower-secondary school (grades 7–9, boys) for the 1405–1406 school year. Pre-registration is open. Parents usually compare several schools, ask other parents in WhatsApp/Telegram groups, visit the school, and look at the school's website and Instagram.

## The school

- Name: Afarinesh non-governmental lower-secondary school for boys (دبیرستان غیردولتی آفرینش، دوره اول پسرانه). Founded 1387 (2008).
- Location: Eram Boulevard, Narvan Street, corner of Alley 14, Shiraz (district 2).
- Hours: Saturday to Wednesday, 07:00–14:00.
- Phones: 071-32293194, 071-32295122, 0917-302-3856, 0917-787-3940. Instagram: afarinesh_sch.
- Slogan on the site: "Big ideas for curious minds". Self-description: a research-oriented school (مدرسه پژوهش‌محور) built on variety, informed choice and attention to individual differences instead of one model for everyone.
- More than 100 students.

## What the website says (sections in order)

1. Hero: night photo of the school's painted wall (red/yellow/blue blocks with black lines) and buttons "Pre-registration 1405–1406" (opens an external Porsline form) and "Request a consultation".
2. Facts: 15–17 students per class; 4 rounds of coordinated exams per year; 3 specialist counsellors (academic, clinical, nutrition); 100+ students.
3. Approach: "from school of life to research-oriented"; motivation, active participation, balanced growth in science, art, skills and social life. Photo of the multi-purpose sports hall.
4. Six strengths: admission interview for every student with parents; experienced and young teachers with constructive interaction and mutual respect; small classes (15–17); maths taught as two subjects (arithmetic, geometry) and science as two (physics–chemistry, biology–earth science); three in-school counsellors; four coordinated exams (multiple-choice and written) plus the school's own exercise books.
5. Elective tracks that run all year, chosen by the student: research (programming, robotics, Kamikar, aerospace, artificial intelligence); arts (drawing, sculpture, graphics, architecture, khatam marquetry, cinema); languages (English with Termic, French); culture (book criticism, Shahnameh reading, creative writing, research methods and article writing); sport (futsal, basketball, volleyball, chess, table tennis); special (creative drama, creativity workshop, life skills, macaroni structures, cuneiform writing on clay).
6. Teachers: 30 staff with photo, role, degrees and experience — founder (law degree, 30+ years teaching and management), principal (MA educational management, 30+ years), deputy, family counsellor (MA psychology, 25 years), maths, sciences, Persian literature, Arabic and Quran, English, social studies (one PhD in Iranian history), aerospace, programming, robotics, graphic design, cinema, sport.
7. FAQ: admission steps (online pre-registration → call from the deputies → visit and specialist interview with parents and student → result and documents); separate sports hall at the end of Yazdanmehr Street with school transport; what makes the teachers special.
8. Registration banner: "Registration for 1405–1406 has started", grades 7, 8 and 9.
9. Contact: address with Google Maps link, phones, email, hours, and a callback form (parent name, mobile, grade, optional message) with a confirmation step.

Not on the site: tuition fees, exam or competition results, olympiad medals, alumni outcomes, parent testimonials, class timetable, transport routes and cost, photos of classes or labs, uniform, lunch, and the dates of open days.

## Kinds of people to include

- Parents of a grade-6 boy who care most about academic results and the entrance exam to selective high schools (Sampad, Shahed, etc.).
- Parents who care most about their son's wellbeing, motivation and personality more than grades.
- Parents with a tight budget who want to know the fee before anything else.
- A parent of a current Afarinesh student who answers questions in a parents' WhatsApp group.
- A grade-6 boy interested in robotics and games.
- A mother who reads every detail on her phone late at night.
- A father who only looks at the site for one minute.
- Parents comparing Afarinesh with a large, well-known private school nearby.
