Afarinesh High School, Shiraz

A private, research-based boys' lower-secondary school (grades 7, 8 and 9) in Shiraz, Iran, since 2008. Every student has his own talents and interests, so instead of one model for everyone, Afarinesh builds several paths of learning and lets each student choose.

A private, research-based school in Shiraz

Afarinesh (in Persian, دبیرستان غیردولتی آفرینش) is a non-governmental boys' high school for the first cycle of secondary education. Its programme rests on variety, informed choice and attention to individual differences: research, art, language, cultural and sports electives are chosen by the students themselves and run until the end of the school year.

  • 15 to 17 students in each class
  • 4 coordinated exams a year
  • 3 specialist counsellors at school: academic, clinical and nutrition
  • 100+ students

Research-based learning

In the research track a student takes an idea from a first sketch to a working build: he writes programs, builds robots and solves problems in a team. Subjects include programming, robotics, aerospace and artificial intelligence.

Specialist teachers and small classes

Each subject is taught by a teacher trained in that field; many hold a master's degree or a doctorate, and several have more than 30 years of teaching experience. Mathematics is taught as two separate courses, arithmetic and geometry, and science as physics-chemistry and biology-earth science.

Electives that last the whole year

  • Research: programming, robotics, aerospace, artificial intelligence
  • Art: drawing, sculpture, graphic design, architecture, khatam (inlay), cinema
  • Languages: English in term courses, and French
  • Cultural: book discussion, Shahnameh reading, creative writing, research methods
  • Sport: futsal and chess
  • Workshops: creative drama, life skills and hands-on making

Admission

Every new student has an interview at school together with his parents. Pre-registration for grades 7, 8 and 9 is done online on the Persian pre-registration form; the school then calls to arrange a visit and interview.
