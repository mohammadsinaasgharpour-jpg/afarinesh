From a school for life to research-based learning

Afarinesh High School is a private boys' lower-secondary school on Narvan Street in Shiraz: a place for curious minds, where students' big ideas are taken seriously. The programme is built on variety, informed choice and attention to each student's differences. A student who takes part in something he cares about develops his talent for real and for good, which is why the research, art, language, culture and sport electives run for the whole school year.

Alongside teaching, three specialist counsellors (academic, clinical and nutrition) work with students in the school itself.

Frequently asked questions

How does admission work?
  1. Parents fill in the pre-registration form (in Persian) for the grade on the school's website.
  2. The vice principals call the family to arrange a visit and an interview.
  3. A specialist interview with the parents and the student takes place at the school.
  4. The result is announced and the family is invited to complete the paperwork.
What kind of school is Afarinesh High School, and where in Shiraz is it?

Afarinesh High School, Shiraz (often called Afarinesh School or Afarinesh Private School) is a private, research-based boys' school for the first cycle of secondary education: grades 7, 8 and 9. Since 2008 it has been in Chenchene, on Narvan Street at the corner of Alley 14, Shiraz, and among the city's private schools it is known for classes of 15 to 17 students and for its research, art, language and sport electives.

How are the electives chosen?

Each student chooses the path he likes from the research, art, language, culture, sport and special strands. These classes are not one-offs; they run until the end of the school year.

How many students are in each class?

Classes have 15 to 17 students, so the teacher can work with every student individually.

How are maths and science taught?

The maths book is taught as two separate courses, arithmetic and geometry, and the science book as two, physics–chemistry and biology–earth science, each by a specialist teacher.

What counsellors does the school have?

Three specialist counsellors work in the school itself: an academic counsellor, a clinical counsellor and a nutrition counsellor.

How are students assessed?

There are four school-wide exam rounds during the year, multiple-choice and written, and students work from the school's own practice books.

Who are the teachers at Afarinesh?

The name, role and degree of every teacher and staff member are listed under Teachers and staff on this page. Maths is taught as arithmetic and geometry, and science as physics–chemistry and biology–earth science.
